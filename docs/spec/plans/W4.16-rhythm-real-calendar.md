## 1. What it does today (read from the code, 2026-09-14)

- **It counts weeks, not taps.** `attendance` holds one row per member per day
  (`attendance_profile_date_unique`), and `recompute_streak` groups those rows into
  distinct Monday-to-Sunday weeks (`rhythm_week`). A Sunday service and a Wednesday
  meeting in the same week are one week. That half of the concern was already right.
- **A run survives one missed week** (a gap of 14 days or less between attended
  weeks), and its length is the number of weeks ATTENDED in it, not the weeks it spans.
- **The week ladder** awards `4_week_rhythm` ("A month of Sundays") at 4 attended weeks
  in the run, then `12_week_rhythm` ("A season with us"), `26` ("Half a year"), `52`
  ("A year of Sundays"), then one per 52 ("N years of Sundays").
- **The app mirrors that ladder** in `milestones.ts` (`nextMilestone`,
  `milestoneFraction`) to draw Home's ring and RHYTHM's Next card ("Next milestone:
  4 weeks", "N to go").
- **Not affected:** the gathering ladder (10, 25, 50, 100, then per hundred), the
  milestone share card (it only uses the badge's title), and the notification centre's
  milestone moments (they go through `badgeFor`).

## 2. Why that is wrong

Every name on the week ladder is a length of calendar time, and every threshold is a
count of attended weeks. They disagree in three ways (dates checked):

1. **A month is 4 or 5 weeks.** November 2026 has five Sundays (1, 8, 15, 22, 29). A
   member who starts on the 1st is told "A month of Sundays" on the 22nd.
2. **Midweek meetings compress it.** Sunday 6 Sep 2026, then Wednesday 9, 16 and 23 Sep,
   fall in four different weeks: four check-ins in 17 days earn the month.
3. **Grace stretches it.** Every other Sunday (6 Sep, 20 Sep, 4 Oct, 18 Oct) is one run
   of 4 weeks, earned after six weeks, and the celebration then says "four Sundays
   running". At the top, 52 attended weeks with grace can take nearly two years.

## 3. Decisions

### 3.1 A month of Sundays: every week of one real calendar month (Ayo)

- **A week belongs to the month its SUNDAY falls in.** So a month has exactly as many
  weeks as it has Sundays (4 or 5), and the week of Mon 28 Sep to Sun 4 Oct 2026
  belongs to October: a check-in on Wed 30 Sep counts toward October.
- **Earned when every week of one calendar month has at least one gathering.** Grace
  does not apply inside that month: a missed week loses that month, and the next month
  is a fresh chance.
- Several gatherings in one week still count once.
- **Any gathering covers its week, not only a Sunday one** (Ayo, settled after planning).
  A week with only a midweek meeting counts toward the month. The rhythm measures showing
  up, not which service somebody came to, and in a diaspora church plenty of members work
  Sunday shifts (nurses, carers, hospitality) and come midweek instead; leaving them out
  would quietly say their faithfulness counts for less. Counting only Sundays would also
  need each branch's service days (`services.weekday` allows any day, and nothing may
  assume Sunday for every branch), and would split a midweek check-in into one that counts
  as a gathering but not toward the month. "A month of Sundays" is a warm name, not a rule
  about the day.
- **Starting mid-month means the next full month counts.** First gathering Sun 20 Sep
  2026: September's weeks of the 6th and 13th are gone, so the month is earned on Sun
  25 Oct 2026 if every October week is covered.
- **Not tied to the current run.** Any fully covered month in the member's history earns
  it, so awarding stays a sweep and stays self-healing across late offline replays.

### 3.2 A season, half a year, a year: real calendar time, grace kept (Ayo)

- **Measured from the first gathering of the CURRENT run.** A run still breaks after two
  missed weeks in a row, exactly as today.
- A season is earned at the first gathering on or after 3 calendar months from that
  date, half a year at 6, a year at 12, then every further 12 months without end.
- Calendar arithmetic is Postgres's (`date + interval 'n months'`), which clamps month
  ends: a run starting 31 Aug reaches its season on 30 Nov.
- **Grace still carries a run across one missed week at a time.** The consequence, stated
  plainly: somebody who gathers every other Sunday for a year earns "A year of Sundays"
  with about 27 gatherings. That is the "a streak is a gift, not a debt" reading chosen
  here. The gathering ladder is unchanged and remains the count of times.
- **On or after the anniversary, never before** (Ayo, settled after planning). A year of
  whole weeks is not a year, so for a Sunday-only member the badge lands 0 to 6 days after
  the date, and the gap shifts from year to year. The alternative, the Sunday nearest the
  anniversary, would sometimes celebrate a year before a year has passed, which is the
  same untruth this item exists to remove. It is also the month's rule: that badge lands
  only once the month is complete.
- Worked example, a run starting Sun 6 Sep 2026: season Sun 6 Dec 2026; half a year Sun
  7 Mar 2027 (6 Mar is a Saturday); a year Sun 12 Sep 2027 (6 Sep 2027 is a Monday, and
  Sun 5 Sep 2027 is one day short, so it does NOT earn it).

### 3.3 Progress shows the weeks left this month (Ayo)

- **Before the month is held,** Home's ring and RHYTHM's Next card count toward it:
  "Next: A month of Sundays", "2 of 4 in September", with the ring and bar filling by
  the month's covered weeks.
- **If a week of the current month has already ended with no gathering,** the target
  moves to next month ("starting October" or similar), with nothing covered yet. The
  ring's label stays the run's week count as today, so there is no big zero, and no copy
  names the miss beyond what the grace copy already does. Exact words are the frames' job.
- **Once the month is held,** they count toward the current run's next time rung in weeks:
  "Next: A season with us", "5 weeks to go".
- **"Next" skips every rung the member already holds** (Ayo, settled after planning). It
  points at the first rung they do not hold yet, measured from the current run's start.
  Today a returning member can be shown "Next: A season with us, 12 weeks to go" while
  holding that badge from an earlier run: the ring fills, they reach it, and nothing
  happens, because `unique(profile_id, kind)` never awards it twice. That is a countdown
  with no celebration at the end, the dead end W2.8 slice 5 already removed once. The month
  comes first while it is not held (a member who gathers every other Sunday can hold a
  season without ever covering a whole month). After it, there is always exactly one such
  time rung, because the time rungs a member holds are always the bottom of that ladder: a
  run that reaches a rung reaches every rung below it, and the sweep awards them together.
  The accepted
  cost: a returning member's next rung may be months away ("Next: Half a year, 26 weeks to
  go"). They still get near-term celebrations from the gathering ladder (10th, 25th, 50th),
  which never resets.
- The strip title and the hero number stay the run's attended-week count ("5-week
  rhythm"). Unchanged.

### 3.4 Decided while planning (reasons recorded, reversible)

- **The kind strings stay.** From here on `4_week_rhythm` MEANS a month of Sundays,
  `12_week_rhythm` a season, `26_week_rhythm` half a year, `52_week_rhythm` a year and
  `52n_week_rhythm` n years: identifiers, not counts. Renaming them (to
  `1_month_rhythm`, say) would need a data migration AND would re-celebrate, because
  `celebrated.ts` keeps each device's list of kinds already told, so a renamed kind reads
  as brand new and throws a party for old news, which `10` rules out. `badgeFor` keeps
  parsing them unchanged. The migration and `milestones.ts` must both say this in a
  comment, or the next reader "fixes" the numbers.
- **Badges already held are kept,** including any the old rule gave that the new one
  would not (`10`: never taken away). No milestone row is deleted or rewritten.
- **The new rules award on the next check-in,** not by backfill. A member whose current
  run has already lasted a season in calendar time without 12 attended weeks is
  celebrated at their next gathering, which is a true celebration. Nobody gains a
  surprise month: a fully covered month means 4 consecutive weeks, which already earned
  `4_week_rhythm` under the old rule.
- **Progress numbers are the server's.** `StreakStrip.tsx` says "every number here is the
  server's", and month progress depends on "today" in the browsed branch's timezone,
  which the client must never decide (`rhythm_state`'s header). So `rhythm_state()`
  returns the progress, and `nextMilestone` / `milestoneFraction` leave the app.

## 4. The build, in slices

### Slice 1 · The database (BE first)

One migration (for example `..._the_rhythm_keeps_a_real_calendar.sql`):

- `rhythm_week_month(date)`: the first day of the month the week's Sunday falls in,
  `date_trunc('month', rhythm_week(d) + 6)`. Pure and immutable, like `rhythm_week`.
- `streaks.run_started_on date`: the first service date of the current run, written by
  `recompute_streak` next to `current_weeks`. A derived cache like the rest of the row;
  make sure the column-privilege inventory treats it as a decision.
- Award helpers that take their inputs as ARGUMENTS, so the edge cases are testable
  without a clock (W3.4's lesson): a full-month check over a member's attendance, and a
  time-rung function `(started date, latest date)` returning `12`, `26`, `52`, `104`...
  for every anniversary reached. Prefer dropping `rhythm_week_rungs` over leaving it
  unused.
- `attendance_after_insert`: award `4_week_rhythm` when any month is fully covered, and
  every time rung the current run has reached. `first_service` and the gathering rungs
  are unchanged.
- `rhythm_state()` and `record_attendance()`: DROP and recreate (a `RETURNS TABLE`
  cannot change shape under `CREATE OR REPLACE`), re-applying their revokes and grants,
  with new columns. Suggested: `next_kind text`, `progress_done integer`,
  `progress_total integer`, `progress_month date` (null unless the next milestone is the
  month). The arithmetic lives in a helper that takes `today` as an argument. `next_kind`
  reads the member's own `milestones` rows so it can skip what they hold (§3.3); both
  functions already answer for `auth.uid()` only, so this adds no new reach.
- **Additive for the builds already installed.** PostgREST returns the new columns as
  extra JSON fields, which builds 22 and 23 ignore. They keep drawing the OLD countdown
  until they update (§5).
- Regenerate `packages/shared/src/database.types.ts`, and run the CI supabase job's four
  steps verbatim before pushing (types-drift is CI-only).

pgTAP: a new file for this item, plus rewriting the assertions in `030` and `031` that
encode the old rule. Those SHOULD go red against the new migration; confirm they do
before rewriting them. Cases, all on fixed dates:

- a 4-Sunday month (Sep 2026) is earned on its 4th Sunday and not before
- a 5-Sunday month (Nov 2026): four weeks do not earn it, the 29th does
- four consecutive weeks straddling two months (20 Sep to 11 Oct 2026) do NOT earn it
- a midweek-only month earns it; a Sunday and a Wednesday in one week count once
- Wed 30 Sep 2026 counts toward October
- every other Sunday never earns the month, however long it goes on
- a mid-month start earns it at the end of the next full month
- season, half a year and a year on their anniversaries with grace weeks inside the run;
  a run broken by two missed weeks restarts the anniversary; the month-end clamp (31 Aug
  to 30 Nov); a second year (`104_week_rhythm`)
- on or after, never before: a run from Sun 6 Sep 2026 does NOT earn the year on Sun 5
  Sep 2027 and does on Sun 12 Sep 2027
- `rhythm_state` progress in each shape: on track this month, this month already lost
  (target next month), the month's last week, month held and counting to a season, lapsed
- a returning member who holds the month and a season from an earlier run is pointed at
  half a year, measured from the new run's start; a member holding a season but not the
  month is pointed at the month
- a recompute never removes a held badge
- `054` (erasure) still reaches `streaks` with the new column
- mutation-check every new assertion: break the rule, watch it fail
