## 1. Why this item exists

The first sermon audio ever put on production went up on 2026-09-05, to film the Play
Console's foreground-service declaration. One evening of real use found six things, and
they are this item:

1. **The manage screen crashed for any message with audio** (Sentry AGBC-DASHBOARD-2):
   `/sermon-audio/[id]` is a Server Component handing a copy bundle with three functions to
   the client-side `ImageField`. Fixed on `fix/dashboard-manage-artwork-boundary` (PR
   pending as this is written). Not part of this item, but the reason its manage screen
   had never been seen on production.
2. **The upload limit the dashboard states is not the one production enforces.** The
   uploader, the copy and the bucket all say 150 MB; the Supabase Free plan refuses anything
   over 50 MB at the storage layer, and the failure came back as "check your connection".
   A 57 MB m4a failed at 22 MB with no true word said about why.
3. **The format advice was a suggestion, and the file that arrived was m4a.** `08` and the
   shelf's own note ask for 64-96 kbps mono MP3. The picker accepted m4a and aac anyway.
4. **Audio-only messages have no home in Watch.** The tab is a hero, Recent messages and
   Recent live streams. An audio-only message is the hero while it is newest, then one row
   among videos, then gone from every rail. Search finds it (title, speaker, series), but
   nobody browsing for something to listen to can see the set.
5. **The player cannot seek except by 15 s, and stops at 1.5x.** Going back to the 30th
   minute of a 96-minute message is 120 taps. A member who listens at 2x cannot.
6. **Leaving the player stops the listening.** The player is created by the sermon
   screen's own hook (`useAudioPlayer` in `useSermonAudio.ts`, line 57) and paused on
   blur (`useFocusEffect`, line 178), so a member who taps back to read the verse of the
   day or check an event loses the message. Backgrounding the whole app keeps it playing;
   staying in the app does not. `08` promises "listen while driving" and says nothing
   about listening while browsing, and the mockup has no frame for it.

## 2. Decisions taken with Ayo (2026-09-06)

- **Audio-only messages get editable facts: title, speaker, series, date preached.** A
  typo can currently only be fixed in the database. Synced YouTube messages stay read-only,
  because the nightly sync owns their facts and would overwrite an edit. **No delete.**
  Removal of an audio-only message stays refused as today (`removeAudio`, reason
  `audio_only`): a message that is nothing at all is not a state we want reachable from a
  button. If a wrong upload has to go, the path is Replace.
- **MP3 only, enforced.** The picker, the mint and the byte check accept `.mp3` /
  `audio/mpeg` and nothing else; the copy says to convert first and how. Decided over
  advise-only because tonight's m4a would then have been refused in the browser with a true
  reason, instead of failing mid-upload with a false one.
- **The size cap is the plan's cap.** `MAX_AUDIO_BYTES` becomes 50 MB and the copy says
  50 MB, with a comment naming the Free-plan limit as the source, so the browser refuses
  before a byte is sent and the words are true. If the project moves to Pro, this constant
  and its copy are the whole change.
- **Watch gets a segmented control: Video / Audio.** Chosen over a third rail and over a
  badge inside Recent messages. The Audio segment lists audio-only messages newest first.
  Search is unchanged in shape (it already matches audio-only messages); its rows show the
  audio cover, which `features/watch/artwork.ts` already decides.
- **Speed goes to 2x, not 3x, and the reason is native.** The installed expo-audio (57.0.3)
  clamps the rate on Android: `AudioPlayer.kt:184`, `rate.coerceIn(0.1f, 2.0f)`. iOS passes
  the rate to AVPlayer unclamped (`AudioModule.swift:223`). Shipping 3x would mean a
  patched native module and an EAS rebuild for a control that would then behave
  differently per platform. Ayo's ask was "3x if possible, otherwise 2x"; the answer is 2x.
  Steps: 1, 1.25, 1.5, 2. Revisit if expo-audio lifts the clamp (check the same line).
- **The scrub bar becomes a seek control**: drag anywhere, tap anywhere, with a knob so it
  looks like one. Built on `PanResponder`, not a native slider, so no dev-client rebuild is
  needed (the dev-client native fence in CLAUDE.md).
- **Listening survives navigation, and a mini player is the way back.** The player moves
  above the screen: one instance, owned by a provider at the root layout for the life of
  the app, and the sermon screen becomes a view of it rather than its owner. While audio
  is loaded, a docked bar sits directly above the tab bar on every tab screen (and above
  the safe area on screens without one): artwork, title, speaker, play/pause, and a
  dismiss that stops and unloads. Tapping the bar opens the sermon screen it came from.
  Opening a message with a YouTube video stops the audio first (`08`: video and audio
  never play together, and background YouTube is a Premium feature we must not imitate).
  Sign-out and account deletion stop and unload it with the rest of a member's state.
  Position keeps ONE owner: the provider writes the throttled local and server position
  exactly as the hook does now, and the mini bar reads it; the sermon screen never
  writes it a second way (the one-fact-one-owner rule in CLAUDE.md, learnt at W2.4).
- **Sequencing agreed before this starts:** (1) the boundary fix PR, (2) the W4.8 docs PR
  (the stashed runbook and `18` edits plus tonight's findings, and this file), (3) the
  in-flight `openExternal` refactor committed by the session that owns it, (4) `main`
  pulled clean, then `feat/w4-9-audio-shelf-and-player` off it.

## 3. Slices

### Slice 1 · The shelf tells the truth and accepts only what plays (dashboard)

- [ ] **The top banner changes subject.** The red "X has no audio yet" `Notice`
      (`Shelf.tsx`, `loadNewestMissing`) goes. In its place, at the top of the shelf, the
      speech-quality guide (`copy.sermonAudio.guideTitle` / `guideBody`), promoted from
      its mid-page slot and extended with the format rule: **MP3 only; if the export is
      m4a, aac or wav, convert it first**, with the one-line `ffmpeg` recipe the guide
      already implies (`-ac 1 -c:a libmp3lame -b:a 64k`). The "newest message without
      audio" fact can stay as a quiet line on that message's row, which already carries
      "NO AUDIO" and "Add audio"; the shelf's counters already show how many are missing.
- [ ] **MP3 only, at every layer that decides.** `ACCEPT` in `AudioUploader.tsx` becomes
      `.mp3,audio/mpeg`; `AUDIO_EXTENSIONS` in `server/sermonAudio.ts` becomes `['mp3']`
      (the mint refuses anything else, so a renamed picker cannot slip a file through);
      `isAudioMagic` drops the `ftyp` branch so an m4a renamed `.mp3` is refused by its own
      bytes; the bucket's `allowed_mime_types` is narrowed to `audio/mpeg` by a migration
      (defence in depth, and the only layer a service-role writer meets). Copy: `dropSub`,
      `pickNotAudio`, and the shelf guide all say MP3.
- [ ] **The cap is 50 MB and the refusal says so.** `MAX_AUDIO_BYTES` = 52 428 800 with a
      comment naming the Free-plan limit; `dropSub`, `pickTooBig` and `guideBody` say
      50 MB. `uploadViaXhr` maps a 413 to the size text and any other 4xx to a "storage
      refused this file" text, and only a network error to "check your connection". The
      bucket's `file_size_limit` comes down to match in the same migration.
- [ ] **Edit the facts of an audio-only message.** On `SERMON-AUDIO-MANAGE`, when
      `youtubeId === null`, a form with title, speaker, series, date preached, prefilled,
      one Save. Server: `updateAudioOnlySermon()` in `server/sermonAudio.ts` performs a
      single UPDATE whose WHERE carries `youtube_id is null`, so a synced row cannot be
      edited even by a crafted request; validation reuses `validateCreate`'s rules
      (title 1-200, date `YYYY-MM-DD`, speaker required). Action `editAudioOnlyAction`
      in `actions.ts` behind `authorize()`, redirecting to the manage screen with an
      outcome line (`OUTCOMES` gains `edited`). No audit row: content edits are not
      privileged actions under ADR 0015 (roles, moderation), and `17` §4 already treats
      the shelf as ordinary admin work. Grants: `sermons` already grants UPDATE to
      `authenticated` under "admins manage sermons", so no migration for the write.
- [ ] **Frames.** `dashboard.html`'s `SERMON-AUDIO-MANAGE` gains the edit block for the
      audio-only variant and loses nothing else; the shelf frame's top banner is redrawn.
      Approved by Ayo before the screen code, like every frame.
- [ ] **Verification.** Vitest `server` project: the mint refuses `m4a`; the byte check
      refuses an `ftyp` file; `updateAudioOnlySermon` edits an audio-only row and leaves a
      synced row untouched (assert the row after, not the return value). Vitest `ui`:
      the picker's `accept`, the 50 MB refusal text, the edit form's prefill and its
      outcome line. pgTAP: the bucket's MIME list and size limit. Then the real thing,
      under `next start` with a minted session (the throwaway-test recipe in the
      dashboard verification memory): upload an m4a and read the refusal; upload an MP3
      over 50 MB and read the refusal; edit a title and see it in the app.

### Slice 2 · Seek and speed (app, player)

- [ ] **Frame first.** `.pl-scrub .bar` in `entry-flow.html` (both W3.1 audio-mode
      frames, light and dark) gains a knob at the played edge: 14 px, gold, the same
      radius token. `05` is synced in the same change. Ayo approves the frame.
- [ ] **Seek.** `AudioTransport.tsx` stops being purely presentational: the bar takes a
      `PanResponder` (grant, move, release) and a tap; both map x to a fraction of the
      laid-out width (`onLayout`) and call `onSeek(fraction * durationSec)`. While a drag
      is live the bar and the clock follow the finger, not the player, and the seek is
      issued once on release, so the player is not asked for a hundred seeks a second.
      `useSermonAudio` exposes `seekTo(sec)` (it already calls `player.seekTo` for resume
      and the 15 s buttons, line 211); the throttled position write and the lock-screen
      metadata update follow a seek exactly as they follow a 15 s tap. The touch target is
      the full bar height plus the `hitSlop` that brings it to 44 px (CLAUDE.md), without
      changing the drawn 5 px.
- [ ] **Accessibility.** The bar gets `accessibilityRole="adjustable"`, a label
      (`watch:scrubLabel`), a value reading the clock, and `accessibilityActions`
      increment / decrement mapped to the existing 15 s steps, so a screen-reader user
      seeks by the same control sighted users drag. Copy keys in all four languages.
- [ ] **Speed.** `SPEEDS` in `features/watch/audio.ts` becomes `[1, 1.25, 1.5, 2]`; the
      cycle button is unchanged. The stored-speed fallback in `nextSpeed` already lands an
      unknown value at 1x. The 2x ceiling and its reason (the Android clamp) are recorded
      in a comment beside the constant.
- [ ] **Verification.** `apps/mobile/src/test/expoAudio.ts` gains `seekTo` recording;
      tests assert a drag to 30% of a 96-minute message issues one `seekTo(1728)` on
      release, that a tap seeks, that the clock follows the finger during a drag, that
      the a11y actions seek by 15 s, and that the speed cycle visits 2 and returns to 1.
      Then the device: seek while playing and while paused, seek past the end (the
      30-second-from-end rule in `08` still applies), lock screen shows the new position,
      both themes, maximum font size with the longest title, and one run with TalkBack.

### Slice 3 · Keep listening while browsing (app, mini player)

- [ ] **Frames first, three of them.** `NOW-PLAYING-BAR` docked above the tab bar, drawn
      on the Home frame (light and dark): 56 px, artwork square at the left, title over
      speaker in the middle, play/pause and dismiss at the right, a 2 px gold progress
      line along its top edge. One frame of it over a screen WITHOUT a tab bar (the
      testimony detail, say) to fix where it sits there. The tablet two-pane `WATCH`
      frame shows it across the rail. Ayo approves all three; `05` gains the pattern.
- [ ] **The player moves up.** A `NowPlayingProvider` in `app/_layout.tsx` owns one
      expo-audio player for the app's life (`createAudioPlayer`, replaced only when a
      different message is loaded, released on sign-out, deletion and dismiss).
      `useSermonAudio` becomes `useNowPlaying()`: the same surface the sermon screen and
      `AudioMode` consume today, backed by the provider instead of a screen-scoped hook.
      The blur pause goes; the AppState background handling, the lock-screen metadata,
      the resume-on-open rule (newer of local and server, `08`) and the throttled
      position writes all move with the player unchanged. The release guard from W3.1
      slice 3 (the try/catch with the reason beside it) stays, now on the provider's
      own unmount and on dismiss.
- [ ] **The bar.** `features/watch/NowPlayingBar.tsx`, rendered by `AppTabBar`'s host so
      it sits above the bar without each tab screen knowing about it, and by the root
      layout on screens without a tab bar. Hidden when nothing is loaded. Tap opens
      `/sermon/[id]` for the loaded message; the sermon screen, finding the provider
      already holds that message, shows it without reloading or seeking. Play/pause is
      the provider's; dismiss stops, writes the final position, unloads. Reduced-motion
      respected on any progress animation. Touch targets 44 px.
- [ ] **Two rules enforced in code, not copy.** Opening a video message calls the
      provider's `stop()` before the YouTube embed mounts. Loading a second audio
      message replaces the first and writes the first's final position before it goes.
- [ ] **Verification.** Unit, against the expo-audio fake: navigating away does not
      pause; the bar appears when audio is loaded and disappears on dismiss; tapping
      the bar routes to the right id; opening a video stops audio; sign-out unloads;

      Device: play, tab to Home, Family, Give and More with the bar present on each;
      open a testimony (no tab bar) and see where the bar sits; lock the phone with the
      bar showing; rotate the tablet; maximum font with the longest title (the bar
      truncates, never grows); TalkBack reads the bar as one control with two actions.
      Then the W3.1 background test again, because the player's owner changed: 15

## 4. Order, gates, and what each slice must not do

- BE-first inside each slice (`25` §1): the mint, the byte check and the update routine
  are proven in Vitest and pgTAP before a form exists for them.
- Frames before screens, approved by Ayo, read first-hand from the mockup in the build
  session, never from this file's description of them (the three-strikes rule in the
  build-from-reference memory).
- A slice is done only after the device matrix, not after green tests: this item exists
  because a green suite never rendered the one screen that mattered.
- Slice 1 must not touch the sync job or synced rows. Slice 2 must not add a native
  dependency. Slice 3 must not leave the sermon screen with a player of its own, and must
  not add a second writer of the position. Slice 4 must not add a query; the filter is on
  data Watch already holds.
- The dashboard redeploys on merge while the bucket migration waits for the manual
  dispatch (`21` §3), so slice 1's PR ends by dispatching `supabase-deploy.yml` and
  checking production's migration head, or its MIME narrowing exists only in the repo.
