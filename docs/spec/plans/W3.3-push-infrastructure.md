# W3.3 · Push infrastructure

Build blueprint for the item that gives the app a voice. Behavioral truth is `15`; the data is
`02`; the jobs side is `21` §5 and ADR 0016. **Delete once W3.3 lands**, moving anything still
load-bearing into the numbered specs, the ADRs and the migrations' own headers (the pattern
W3.1's plan set).

This item is two things wearing one number, and the second is easy to miss:

1. **Push infrastructure**: the `notifications` log, the ticket ledger, a sender that carries
   generic payloads only, a receipts sweep that prunes dead tokens, six Android channels, token
   registration, deep links that survive a cold killed start, and the two screens (`NC`,
   `NOTIF-PREFS`).
2. **MORE's member "My life" section.** W1.2 built the hub with a guest sign-in card standing in
   its place, and `apps/mobile/app/(tabs)/more.tsx` line 32 still says so in a comment. Four of
   the five destinations already exist (`app/settings/profile.tsx`, `rhythm.tsx`, `my-list.tsx`,
   `my-posts.tsx`); `NC` is the one this item builds, which is why the section lands here.

## Decisions taken with Ayo (2026-08-15, before any code)

### 1. `notifications` is NOT partitioned

`02` line 468 raised this as an open conflict for W3.3 to resolve deliberately, and it is
resolved by dropping the partitioning rather than by working around it.

Postgres requires every unique constraint on a partitioned table to include the partition key.
`unique(profile_id, broadcast_id)` and the partial unique on `(profile_id, dedupe_key)` are the
mechanism that stops a fan-out re-run and a rescheduled reminder from double-notifying a member,
so a version of them that only holds inside one month is not the guarantee `02` was asking for:
a fan-out retried across midnight on the 1st would notify everyone twice, and that is the
failure the constraint exists to prevent.

The alternative that keeps both (partitions plus an unpartitioned side table holding the dedupe
keys) is strictly more machinery for the same guarantee: two writes per notification, a
partition-creation job, and a second retention clock.

**What partitioning was buying was retention, and at this church's volume it is not worth its
own failure mode.** Realistic ceiling: ~2,000 members × ~12 notifications a month ≈ 24k rows a
month, ~290k rows standing under the 12-month retention. A monthly batched DELETE over that is
sub-second. Against it, a partition-maintenance job that misses its run makes INSERTS fail at
midnight on the 1st, which for this table means reminders silently stop: the canonical failure
`21` §5 names at the top of its own job table. The asymmetry decides it. A retention job that
fails leaves stale rows; a partition job that fails breaks sending.

Revisit if the table ever passes a few million rows. `02` and `21` §5 are corrected in the
slice-2 PR, and the reasoning gets **ADR 0022** so the next reader finds the decision rather
than the gap.

### 2. NOTIF-PREFS keeps five toggles; "Prayer activity" owns two columns

The frame draws five switches and captions the prayer one "When someone prays with you, and
reminders to pray", so it already said it covers both. That is a design intent, not an
oversight, and it wins: one control writes both `prayer_activity` and `prayer_reminders`.
`15`'s tier table (which gives `prayer_reminders` its own pref key) is corrected to say the two
columns share one control, and the columns stay separate in the schema so a later item can
split the control without a migration.

Cost, stated plainly: a member who likes "three people prayed with you" but finds the nudges
naggy has to lose both.

### 3. Only service reminders interrupt

Android channel importance is effectively immutable once created (the OS remembers a deleted
channel's settings), so this is a one-shot call and it is made here rather than in passing.

| Channel | Pref key | Importance | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| `service_reminders` | `service_reminders` | HIGH | "Service starts in 1 hour" is useless seen late; it is the one category where arriving after the fact is a failure |
| `ministry` | `ministry_announcements` | DEFAULT | Sound, no takeover |
| `branch` | `branch_updates` | DEFAULT | Sound, no takeover |
| `prayer` | `prayer_activity` (+ `prayer_reminders`) | DEFAULT | The wedge's reward loop: felt, not intrusive |
| `testimony` | `testimony_activity` | DEFAULT | As above |
| `transactional` | none (always on) | DEFAULT | Answers something the member did |

All six are created at first app start, BEFORE any permission request: Android 13+ will not show
the permission prompt until a channel exists (`15`). Members keep per-channel control in
Android's own settings regardless.

### 4. The WhatsApp section is struck from NOTIF-PREFS

Not a judgement call: ADR 0014 removed WhatsApp broadcasts on 2026-07-29 and migration
`20260729140000` dropped `notification_prefs.whatsapp_opt_in` with them. The frame still draws
the toggle, so the frame is stale and gets corrected in slice 1.

### 5. MORE's member hub opens on who you are

Two variants were composed and rendered in both themes before the choice. The one not taken put
Home's rhythm band in the sign-in card's slot, which the guest frame's own name ("no rhythm
band") had anticipated and which would have cost no new CSS. It lost because it repeats what
Home already shows one tab away, while an identity card says the one thing MORE is the only
place to say. `.mehead` inherits `.signin`'s ink, margin and radius exactly, so the guest and
member hubs sit identically; the avatar is `.profhead .pa`'s gradient at 46px.

The card and the "Profile" row both open `PROFILE`, deliberately: the card is a glance, the row
is a destination. The rejected variant is deleted from the mockup rather than kept as clutter.

### 6. A band's hairline is a token now, not a patch

Found while rendering the frames in dark, and confirmed by reading the values rather than the
picture: `band` is `palette.ink` `#0e1420` in BOTH themes, and dark `bg` is `#0e1420` too. An
unbordered ink band therefore has literally no edge at night and stops reading as a surface.

The app had already hit this twice and fixed it in place twice, in `StatusPanel` (2026-08-08,
seen on the phone) and `StreakHero`, both writing `name === 'dark' ? colors.cardline :
'transparent'` inline. Three other surfaces drawing a band never got the patch: the guest
sign-in card, the `Toast` (the worst case, since it floats over a screen and carries no shadow
either), and this item's new `.mehead`.

So it is a token: `colors.bandline`, `transparent` in light and `#28323f` in dark, always
applied so a band measures identically in both themes and only its colour changes. `05` and the
mockup's `--bandline` are synced in the same change, and the two in-place patches now read the
token. Deliberately its own name rather than an alias of `cardline`: a card's hairline and a
band's answer to different surfaces and either may move without the other.

Verified on the physical Android in both themes: the sign-in card and the toast gain a visible
edge in dark and are pixel-unchanged in light. `StatusPanel` and `StreakHero` are unchanged by
construction (the token's value is exactly the expression they used).

Not given a hairline, on purpose: the splash (`app/index.tsx`) is a full-screen ink page with
nothing behind it to separate from, and the player's media box (`app/sermon/[id].tsx`) always
draws a player, artwork or the branded gradient inside itself, so it never presents as a bare
ink rectangle.

### Slice 2 · the tables

- `notifications`: plain table, both unique rules as `02` writes them, `deep_link` holding an
  expo-router path, `template_key` + `params` for automated rows and `title`/`body` for manual
  broadcast rows only.
- `push_tickets`: `ticket_id` PK, `device_id`, `sent_at`, `processed_at`. Purged after 7 days.
- RLS per `02`'s matrix row: anon none, member own rows, admin all. Nobody writes their own
  notifications: rows arrive from the service role, so there is no client INSERT grant at all.
  The one client write is `read_at`, and it is a column-scoped UPDATE grant, not a table-level
  one (the `20260803140000` lesson: RLS chooses rows and never columns).
- Read path: keyset cursor on `(created_at desc, id desc)`, ~30 a page. Mark-read and
  mark-all-read as scoped updates.
- pgTAP: the policy matrix, both dedupe constraints proven by a duplicate insert failing, a
  member unable to write another member's `read_at`, and a member unable to write anything on
  their own row except `read_at`.

### Slice 3 · the sender and the receipts

Follows ADR 0016 exactly; nothing here is a new pattern.

- **Rendering:** automated notifications store a template key + params. The PUSH payload is
  rendered server-side in the recipient's `profiles.language`; the NC row is rendered
  client-side by i18next from the same key. Edge functions cannot import `packages/shared` (no
  path in `supabase/functions/deno.json`), so the server-side strings live in
  `supabase/functions/_shared/`. Payloads stay generic: never testimony or prayer content, on
  the lock screen or in a log (`15`, `20`).
- **`push-receipts`** job: `cron.schedule` in its own migration calling
  `jobs.invoke_edge_function('push-receipts')`, every 15 minutes. Lease, sweep ALL unprocessed
  tickets (Expo keeps receipts only ~24h), delete `devices` rows on `DeviceNotRegistered`,
  alert when more than 10% of a day's automated tickets error, record what it processed only
  after processing, release the lease, ping healthchecks (`HEALTHCHECK_URL_PUSH_RECEIPTS`) and
  ping FAILURE when it could not deliver.

### Slice 4 · the device half

- Channels before permission; token registration only after the in-context grant (`06`), never
  for a guest (`02`/`15`); the row deleted on sign-out.
- Deep links: `agbcglobal://` is already in `app.config.js`. This slice adds
  `android.intentFilters` with `autoVerify: true` and `ios.associatedDomains`, plus the response
  listener and `getLastNotificationResponseAsync` for a cold killed start. **Navigate only**: a
  deep link never carries or triggers a write (`03`'s gate-return security rule), so the target
  goes through an allowlist, exactly as the pending-action store does.
- The AASA and `assetlinks.json` files are served by the church website, so they are a PR
  against `Desktop/agbc`, not this repo. The Android fingerprint is the **Play App Signing**
  SHA-256 already recorded in `19` line 16, never the upload keystore.
- `docs/runbooks/credentials.md` line 134 is this slice's: add the EAS dev/preview keystore
  SHA-1s to the Firebase Android key restriction, or the first real push 403s.
- Analytics: `broadcast_received`, `broadcast_opened`, `notification_opened` flip from
  `deferred` to `app` in `packages/shared/src/analytics/events.ts`.

### Slice 5 · the screens

`NC` (cursor pagination, activity batching, badge cap at 99+, retention footer, four states),
`NOTIF-PREFS` (five toggles on the shared `ToggleRow`/`ToggleList` W2.10 slice 1 built for
exactly this, plus the OS-denied banner), MORE's "My life", and the Home bell, which
`home.tsx` line 343 currently hides from members with a comment naming this item.

## What is NOT this item

- **Broadcasts** (compose, four-eyes approval, fan-out, `broadcast_deliveries`) are W3.5. This
  item builds the road; W3.5 drives the first big convoy down it.
- **The reminder jobs themselves** (service reminders, RSVP reminders, prayer reminders,
  retention purges) are W3.4. This item provides the sender and the dedupe guarantee they use.
- **The two blocked Sentry DSNs** wait on infrastructure that does not exist yet
  (`docs/runbooks/credentials.md` §Observability): the dashboard's at its first Vercel deploy,
  the edge one at the `19` cutover.
- **iOS provisional authorization** for service reminders is a "consider" in `15`, not a
  requirement, and there is no iPhone in the project to verify it on. Noted, not built.
