W3.1 · SERMON AUDIO · admin only, the weekly shelf

The "listen while driving" promise (08) exists only for a message whose MP3 was actually shelved, and this is where the shelf is fed: one upload per service, every week, a standing media-team task (18 assigns its owner before launch). YouTube cannot substitute: background listening is a Premium feature and pulling the audio out of a video breaks YouTube's terms. The bucket is private and the app mints 24-hour signed links (decision 2026-08-14), so a copied URL dies within a day. Two flows live here: attach audio to a synced message, and create a message that was never on YouTube at all. Slice 5 adds the picture. A message created here had none anywhere: the sync writes thumbnail_url for what it finds on YouTube and nothing writes it for what it does not, so a midweek word was a blank navy card in the rails, a blank cover on the player and no art at all on a lock screen. The artwork is its own object in its own bucket (artwork_path, never thumbnail_url, which the nightly sync owns and would overwrite), it is optional everywhere, and unlike the audio it is public-read: it is the picture on every card in a guest-first app, so there is nothing in it to fence and a rotating signed URL would defeat the image cache it depends on.
