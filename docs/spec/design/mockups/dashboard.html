W2.7 slice 4 · DAILY VERSES · admin only, four languages, batch first

The one Phase A surface with an outside clock on it (22 §1): a comms volunteer queues a quarter at a time, and the failure when they do not is silent. The app asks for the newest verse dated on or before today, per language, so an empty day never blanks the card: it repeats an older verse instead. Nobody reports a bug, the daily touchpoint just quietly dies. Every decision below follows from that one fact, which is why depth is counted PER LANGUAGE and never as a single number.
