What the screens draw from. Every value above comes from rhythm_state(branch) and record_attendance(branch): today, checked_in, state, current_weeks, longest_weeks, last_service_date. The app never decides what day it is and never keeps a checked-in flag of its own, so the card and the write cannot disagree. Milestones and attendance history are own-row selects.

The toast (no frame: the app's Toast primitive, announced through a live region). First tap: “You're here. Lovely to have you.” A tap that recorded nothing because the day already had a row, which happens on a second device or when the offline queue replays: “You're already checked in.” Offline: the tap is queued, the card shows checked-in immediately and the toast says “Saved. We'll record this when you're back online.”

Milestone copy, one headline per kind the backend awards: first_service “Your first Sunday with us” 🎉 · 4_week_rhythm “Four weeks of rhythm” 🔥 · 12_week_rhythm “Twelve weeks of rhythm” 🕊 · first_testimony “Your first testimony is live” ✦ · first_prayer “Your first prayer request is live” 🙏. The overlay is shown once per milestone, when the app first sees a row it has not celebrated.

📈 W2.10 · Analytics consent · light

FLOW  ·  onboarding ends → Home (guest) → the ask, once → answered either way, and never asked again. The switch itself lives in SETTINGS › Privacy & data, edited in place in the Light mode gallery below: Settings is one screen, not two references.

Four decisions this frame records (ADR 0020, with `20` amended in the same change). Opt-in, not anonymous-for-everyone: nothing product-analytics fires until somebody says yes, which is what lets the events carry a lasting device id and therefore what makes weekly contributing members and week-4 retention computable at all (`22` §5). Asked once, on the first Home, as a guest: first run has no account, so consent is stored on the DEVICE and works signed out; “No thanks” is a complete answer and Settings is the only way back, because a second ask is nagging. No account link ever: events carry branch, scope, language and role, never the member id, so a phone and a tablet count as two people and that inflation is accepted in exchange for holding less about identifiable people under Art. 9. Crash reports are not gated and are always sent with name, email and content scrubbed: the staged-rollout halt criterion in `21` §8 is meaningless if it only measures the members who opted in.

🎧 W3.1 · Audio mode · light

FLOW  ·  SERMON (video) → tap Audio only → the embed unmounts and these take its place. A message that was never on YouTube opens in the right-hand state directly. The video state itself is edited in place in the Light mode gallery below: SERMON is one screen, not four references.

Two decisions this pair records (taken with Ayo 2026-08-14). The artwork is the message's own thumbnail, dimmed: a scrim, because the picture is a video frame and nothing is playing there. A message that was never on YouTube has no thumbnail at all (the dashboard inserts those rows with thumbnail_url empty, sermonAudio.ts), so it falls back to the branded gradient the player already uses, and the same fallback is what the lock screen gets. Amended by W3.1 slice 5 (2026-08-15): that fallback is no longer the only thing such a message can show. The dashboard can now give any message its own artwork, held in its own column and its own bucket, and the app prefers it over the YouTube thumbnail everywhere the two could both exist: here, on every rail and search row, on every MY-LIST row, and on the lock screen. The gradient stays exactly where it was, as the fallback for a message with neither. The scrim stays too, over ours as much as theirs: the gold disc and the state pill sit on top of it and have to stay readable over a photograph nobody has seen yet. Speed took the Download control's place: 08 specifies play/pause/seek, ±15s, speed, notes, save, share and open-on-YouTube, and never download; that control had sat dimmed since W1.3, and speed had no home anywhere in the mockup. The transport belongs to audio alone: while the YouTube embed is up it draws its own controls (W1.3), so the scrub, the ±15s pair and speed all arrive with this mode and are gone without it. The controls themselves were redrawn on 2026-08-14 (W3.1 slice 4, direction A of three shown to Ayo): the row of three identical tiles gave one shape to a MODE, a VALUE and a DESTINATION, so mode became a segmented Video/Audio control that says what it is, and Speed and Notes became quiet actions beneath it. Speed is absent rather than dimmed while the video plays; the segment above it is the answer to "why". The old .pl-tiles row still dresses the tablet frame, which belongs to a later item (LIVE was cut by ADR 0021 on 2026-08-15).

Amazing Grace

Our story

Amazing Grace Bible Church began in Glasgow and has grown into a family spread across nations, joined by one grace and one Lord.

What we believe

The Bible is our foundation, Jesus is our Saviour, and the Holy Spirit is our helper. We gather to worship, grow, and carry one another.

Our mission

To reach every city God sends us to, and to make every member feel at home in the family, wherever they are in the world.

The Grace Awakening

Chapter 4 · Grace that finds you

Grace does not wait at the finish line for you to arrive. It runs out to meet you while you are still a long way off, still rehearsing the speech you think you need to earn your way back in.

That is the scandal at the centre of the gospel. Not that God loves the improved version of you, but that he loved you first, before a single thing was fixed, and called you his own.

So today, before you do anything, before you pray a better prayer or keep a better promise, hear this: you are already found. The searching is over. What remains is simply to be carried home.

Day 8
Today's verse
But God commends his own love toward us, in that while we were yet sinners, Christ died for us.
Romans 5:8 (WEB)
Reflection

Grace doesn't wait for you to get it right. Before a single thing was fixed in you, love had already moved toward you, and made its home with you.

You are not loved because you performed. You are loved, and out of that love you get to live. Let that reorder your day.

Prayer
Father, thank you that I am loved before I perform. Help me rest in that today, and love others from the same grace. Amen.

The Grace Awakening

Chapter 4 · Grace that finds you

Grace does not wait at the finish line for you to arrive. It runs out to meet you while you are still a long way off, still rehearsing the speech you think you need to earn your way back in.

That is the scandal at the centre of the gospel. Not that God loves the improved version of you, but that he loved you first, before a single thing was fixed, and called you his own.

So today, before you do anything, before you pray a better prayer or keep a better promise, hear this: you are already found. The searching is over. What remains is simply to be carried home.

This is where rest begins. Not in the striving, not in the proving, but in the quiet confidence that the God who started this will be the one to finish it.
